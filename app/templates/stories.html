Twelve Ponds and a Village That Stayed

In a drought-prone block of Rajasthan, families were packing up for seasonal migration — again. TPF restored twelve traditional ponds: de-silting, reinforcing bunds, and training village maintenance committees. Two years later, our team returned to find the committees still active — collecting user fees, maintaining catchments, and managing repairs entirely on their own. The village didn't just get water. It got ownership.

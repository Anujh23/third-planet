Who We Are

Third Planet Foundation is a Section 8 not-for-profit that was born from a simple belief: CSR funding should create real, lasting change — not just fill compliance reports. Since 2012, we've been on the ground, partnering with companies and communities to co-create development programmes that people can touch, feel, and own.

We work across India's most underserved regions, covering the complete CSR lifecycle — from policy formulation and need assessment through direct implementation to impact reporting. Every programme is aligned to Schedule VII, Section 135 of the Companies Act 2013 and the Sustainable Development Goals.

Our focus is simple: development that communities participate in, benefit from, and eventually lead themselves.

Our Process

From the first conversation to the final impact report — here's how we work

We handle the complete CSR lifecycle so you can focus on what matters. Every step is designed for transparency, compliance, and — most importantly — real outcomes in real communities.

From need assessment to impact reporting, our process is built on community participation, rigorous monitoring, and honest accountability.

1

CSR Policy & Strategy

We start by understanding your company's vision and formulating a CSR policy aligned with Schedule VII, Section 135 of the Companies Act 2013. This isn't just compliance paperwork — it's a roadmap for meaningful investment.

2

Need Assessment & Baseline

Before we design anything, we go to the community. Participatory assessments, baseline surveys, and gap analyses help us understand what's actually needed — not what we assume from a distance.

3

Programme Design

Evidence-based programme design with a clear theory of change, measurable KPIs, and community participation baked into every stage. We plan for sustainability from day one.

4

Implementation

Our field teams execute directly — no layers of sub-contractors. Transparent budgets, regular progress reporting, and community members involved as partners, not just beneficiaries.

5

Monitoring & Evaluation

Real-time dashboards, field reviews, and third-party evaluations keep every project on track. When something isn't working, we course-correct immediately — not at the year-end review.

6

Impact Reporting

Comprehensive impact assessments, social audits, and statutory CSR reporting — fully compliant and fully transparent. Every number tells a story; every story has a name behind it.

Ready to Start?

We'll walk you through every step — from the first conversation to your first impact report.

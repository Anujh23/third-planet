Rajasthan — Water Conservation

Restored 12 traditional ponds across the semi-arid belt — de-silted, bund-reinforced, with trained village maintenance committees. Two years on, the committees still maintain them independently.

Environment

Uttar Pradesh — Girl Child Education

After-school Learning Labs with trained local tutors improved reading outcomes across multiple villages. First-generation learners now read fluently — some tutors run the programme independently.

Education

Madhya Pradesh — Clean Energy

Women's SHGs trained to sell and maintain improved cookstoves in their own villages — cutting fuel use, reducing indoor smoke, and earning income for SHG members.

Clean Energy

Odisha — Mobile Health Camps

Preventive care and nutrition screening reached 40+ remote hamlets. Over 3,000 people received check-ups, referrals, and follow-up care.

Healthcare

Jharkhand — Women in Governance

Three women from a tribal block won panchayat seats after our leadership mentorship and now champion girl child education in their wards.

Empowerment

Meghalaya — Indigenous Farming

Partnered with NESFAS to support indigenous farming practices, protecting biodiversity while improving farmer incomes through direct market access.

Agriculture

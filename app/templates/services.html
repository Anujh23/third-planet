Frequently Asked Questions

What is Section 135 of the Companies Act?

Section 135 requires companies with a net worth of Rs 500 crore or more, turnover of Rs 1,000 crore or more, or net profit of Rs 5 crore or more to spend at least 2% of their average net profit on CSR activities. Third Planet Foundation helps companies identify, design, implement, and report these programmes in compliance with Schedule VII.

What thematic areas do you cover under Schedule VII?

We implement programmes across education, healthcare, women empowerment, skill development, water & sanitation (WASH), rural development, environmental sustainability, and disaster management — all aligned to Schedule VII of the Companies Act 2013.

How long does a typical CSR project take?

Timelines vary by scope. A focused intervention (health camps, awareness drives) can be executed in 2-3 months. Multi-year programmes like education labs or livelihood development typically run 12-36 months. We provide detailed timelines during the proposal stage.

Do you work across all Indian states?

We have active presence in 20+ states and union territories. Our field teams operate in both urban and rural settings — from Delhi NCR to remote tribal blocks in Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, and Rajasthan. View our presence map.

How do you measure impact?

Every programme follows a baseline → midline → endline assessment framework. We track both outputs (activities delivered) and outcomes (lives changed) using quantitative KPIs and qualitative community feedback. Corporate partners receive detailed impact reports with third-party verification where applicable.

Our Story

From a conversation in a Delhi office to programmes across 20+ states

Every organisation begins with a moment of conviction. For Third Planet Foundation, it was a simple question: what if CSR could be about people first and paperwork second?

What began as a small team in a Delhi office has grown into a pan-India movement — 500+ projects, 20+ states, and a million lives touched. Here's how we got here.

2012
Where It Began

A Founding Conviction

A small team of development practitioners registered Third Planet Foundation as a Section 8 not-for-profit in New Delhi, driven by the belief that CSR funding could do more than tick compliance boxes — it could change lives.

2014
The CSR Mandate

Section 135 Changes Everything

The Companies Act 2013 made CSR spending mandatory for qualifying companies. Overnight, the need for credible, ground-level implementation partners became urgent. TPF was already in the field, ready.

2016
Growing Footprint

Expanding Across States

From Delhi-NCR to Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, and Uttar Pradesh — our field teams began working in 10+ states. Education centres opened, health camps reached remote hamlets, and the first SHGs formed under our programmes.

2018
Recognition & Trust

Credentials That Matter

Empanelment on NITI Aayog's Darpan portal, GuideStar India certification, and GeM registration validated what communities already knew — TPF delivers what it promises.

2020–21
When It Mattered Most

COVID-19 Response

When the pandemic struck, we pivoted overnight — distributing relief kits during the first wave and running free vaccination drives with The Muthoot Group during the devastating second wave across Delhi, Gurgaon, and Faridabad. Crisis revealed character.

2023
Pan-India Presence

20+ States, 500+ Projects

Today, we work across 20+ states and union territories with 25+ corporate partners. From water conservation in Rajasthan to girl child education in West Bengal, from STEM labs in six cities to youth training in Ladakh — every project is a story of community partnership.

Now
The Road Ahead

Deepening Our Impact

We're investing in technology-enabled monitoring, climate resilience programmes, and capacity building for the next generation of development leaders. The best chapters are still being written — by communities themselves.

"We didn't set out to become the biggest. We set out to be the most trusted — the kind of partner a sarpanch calls first, not because of a contract, but because of a relationship."
— Founding Team, Third Planet Foundation

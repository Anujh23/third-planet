Our Mission

Every life deserves dignity, every community deserves a chance to thrive

We started with a simple belief — that no one should be left behind in India's growth story. Not the farmer watching his borewell run dry. Not the girl walking five kilometres past a school she cannot afford. Not the mother choosing between medicine and a meal. Third Planet Foundation exists because these stories don't have to end this way.

Our Vision

A Resilient, Prosperous India

We see an India where development is not a top-down programme but a movement built from the ground up — where villages manage their own water, women lead their own enterprises, children learn with curiosity instead of fear, and every community has the knowledge, resources, and voice to shape its own future.

This is not charity. This is partnership. We walk alongside communities until they no longer need us — and that is the only measure of success we recognise.

"To build a resilient and prosperous nation by championing sustainable development, fostering inclusive growth, and empowering communities for a better tomorrow."
Our Mission

Turning Intention Into Impact

India does not lack compassion. It does not lack resources. What it often lacks is the disciplined bridge between the two — the ability to take a corporate's desire to give back and translate it into a programme that actually changes lives on the ground.

That is our work. We design, implement, and monitor CSR programmes that are rooted in evidence, shaped by the communities they serve, and built to outlast our involvement. Every rupee is accounted for. Every outcome is measured. Every promise is kept.

"To drive holistic socio-economic development by empowering communities, fostering sustainable growth, and creating opportunities that transform lives."
What We Stand For

The Commitments That Guide Us

Behind every programme, every partnership, and every field visit — these are the promises we never break.

Dignity Before Everything

We never treat communities as beneficiaries. They are partners, decision-makers, and the true experts of their own lives. Every programme begins with listening — deeply, patiently, and without assumptions.

Discipline in Execution

Good intentions fail without rigorous execution. We bring structured project management, transparent budgets, milestone tracking, and honest reporting to every initiative — because the communities we serve deserve nothing less.

Sustainability Over Spectacle

We don't chase ribbon-cuttings. We build capacity. Every programme includes an exit plan — training local leaders, forming community committees, and ensuring the work continues long after we step back.

The Change We Seek

Where Our Energy Goes

We focus where the need is deepest and where disciplined effort can create lasting transformation.

Education & Learning

From STEM labs in government schools to digital literacy in remote districts — we believe every child deserves the tools to dream bigger than their circumstances allow.

Healthcare & Wellbeing

Maternal health camps, nutritional screening, community health workers — we bring preventive care to doorsteps that ambulances have never reached.

Women Empowerment

Self-help groups, leadership training, micro-enterprise support — when women lead, families prosper, villages transform, and the next generation inherits a different world.

Water & Sustainability

Borewell recharge, watershed restoration, community water governance — because water is not just a resource, it is the foundation on which every other development goal stands.

Guiding Principles

What Drives Every Decision

Four principles that shape how we design, deliver, and measure everything we do.

Community First

Listen deeply, respect local knowledge, and strengthen local capacity. The best solutions already exist in the communities we serve — our job is to unlock them.

Evidence-Led

Data-driven decisions, research-backed interventions, and continuous feedback loops. We measure what matters and course-correct when the numbers demand it.

Equity & Inclusion

Prioritising women, youth, tribal communities, and the most vulnerable in every programme we design — because impact must reach those who need it most, not those who are easiest to reach.

Climate-Smart

Promoting resilience, regenerative practices, and sustainable solutions. Every intervention considers the environment it operates in — and the one it leaves behind.

A Promise

We Will Not Stop

As long as there are villages without clean water, schools without teachers, women without opportunity, and children without hope — our work is not done. We carry this responsibility not as a burden but as a privilege. Every community that trusts us with their future makes us more determined, more disciplined, and more committed to getting it right.

This is not a job for us. It is a calling. And we show up for it — every single day.

About Us

Partnering with Companies & Communities for Sustainable Change

In 2012, a small team set out with a simple belief: that every CSR rupee should translate into something a community can touch, feel, and own. Third Planet Foundation was born from that conviction — a Section 8 not-for-profit that partners with companies and communities to co-create development programmes that actually last.

Today, we provide end-to-end, Table-to-Field CSR services — from planning and execution to reporting — covering the complete lifecycle, aligned to Schedule VII, Section 135 of the Companies Act 2013 and the Sustainable Development Goals. But behind every compliance number is a village that got its first library, a woman who started her first enterprise, a child who stayed in school.
